Delete your WipNest data

App: WipNest (Android) · Developer: Aldrin Maquiling
Contact: maquiling.aa@gmail.com

WipNest has no accounts. Your projects, plans and notes are stored only on your phone, so you can delete them yourself at any time. Nobody else holds a copy unless you made a backup.

Delete projects in the app

  1. Open the project, tap the ⋮ menu, then Delete. The project moves to Recently Deleted.
  2. To remove it right away, go to Settings → Recently Deleted and tap Delete permanently. Otherwise it is erased automatically after 30 days.

Delete everything on your phone

Uninstall WipNest, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → WipNest → Storage → Clear storage. This erases all projects, plans, notes, settings and the saved Pro purchase confirmation.

Delete your Google Drive backup (Pro)

  1. Open drive.google.com on a computer, signed in to the Google account you connected.
  2. Click the gear icon → Settings → Manage apps.
  3. Find WipNest, click Options → Delete hidden app data.

To also stop WipNest's access, tap Disconnect account in Settings → Backups, or remove WipNest at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Backup files you saved

Backup files you exported are ordinary files on your phone or wherever you saved them. Delete them like any other file.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted by the steps above: all project data (projects, plans, notes, history), app settings, the on-device Pro purchase confirmation and, if you use Pro, your Drive backup.
  • Kept by WipNest: nothing. The Pro verification service does not store your purchase. Google Cloud keeps basic request logs (time, IP address and result) for up to 30 days to run and secure the service, then deletes them automatically.
  • Kept by Google Play: your Pro purchase record stays with your Google account under Google Play's own policies, so you can restore Pro later.
