Closed testing on Google Play

Help WipNest hatch

Before WipNest can be released to everyone, Google needs 12 people to test it for 14 days. It takes about a minute, and it would really help.

  • Android only
  • Free to test
  • About a minute

Three steps, in this order

Use the same Google account for all three: the one signed in to the Play Store on your phone.

  1. 1

    Join the tester group

    Google Play only lets members of this group in, so this comes first.

    Join the group
  2. 2

    Tap "Become a tester"

    This opens Google Play's testing page. Accept the invitation there.

    Open the testing page
  3. 3

    Download the app

    If the Play Store says "not found", do step 2 first and give it a few minutes.

    Get it on Google Play

Then just keep it for 14 days

Keep WipNest on your phone and open it once in a while. That's all Google needs.

Keep it for 14 days

Leave it on your phone for the full two weeks and stay in the tester group.

Add a real project

If you're up for it, add that one project you've been meaning to finish.

Tell me what's confusing

Honest feedback is very welcome. Anything that felt unclear helps.
