Free and Pro

WipNest is a personal project tracker. The free version supports unlimited Ideas, up to 10 started projects (any status other than Idea, archived ones included) and manual file backups. Pro is a one-time, non-renewing purchase through Google Play that unlocks unlimited started projects and optional manual Google Drive backup on Android. The actual price is shown by Google Play before purchase. A purchase through Google Play does not transfer to another app store. Google Play handles payment and refund requests under its applicable terms.

Your data

Your project data belongs to you. You are responsible for keeping a usable backup if you move devices, uninstall the app or replace local data. File and Drive restores replace the current local WipNest project data; they do not merge device histories. Review the snapshot and export current data before confirming a restore. Google Drive backup is a user-initiated snapshot, not continuous sync.
